Short Scale of Creative Self

This questionnaire evaluates two dimensions of your creative self-concept : your creative identity (how central creativity is to who you are) and your creative self-efficacy (your confidence in tackling creative challenges).

There are no right or wrong answers — respond spontaneously and honestly.
